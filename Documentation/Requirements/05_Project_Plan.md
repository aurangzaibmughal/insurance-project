## 6. Risk Management

### Risk 1: Technical Challenges
- **Risk:** Difficulty in payment gateway integration
- **Impact:** High
- **Probability:** Medium
- **Mitigation:** Research payment gateway APIs early, use sandbox for testing

### Risk 2: Time Constraints
- **Risk:** Project may take longer than estimated
- **Impact:** High
- **Probability:** Medium
- **Mitigation:** Prioritize core features, work on critical path items first

### Risk 3: Requirement Changes
- **Risk:** Requirements may change during development
- **Impact:** Medium
- **Probability:** Low
- **Mitigation:** Get requirement approval early, document all changes

### Risk 4: Testing Issues
- **Risk:** Bugs discovered late in development
- **Impact:** Medium
- **Probability:** Medium
- **Mitigation:** Implement unit tests early, continuous testing
