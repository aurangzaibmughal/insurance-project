# Problem Definition

---

## 1. Background

Insurance is a contract for payment of a sum of money to the person assured on the happening of the event insured against. The insurance industry requires efficient management systems to handle various types of insurance policies, customer information, premium calculations, and payment processing.

Currently, many insurance companies face challenges in:
- Manual processing of insurance applications
- Difficulty in tracking policy details
- Complex premium calculations
- Limited accessibility for policyholders
- Inefficient loan processing for policyholders

---

## 2. Problem Statement

There is a need for an **Online Insurance Management System** that can:

1. Provide information about various insurance policies (Life, Medical, Motor, Home)
2. Allow new users to register and existing users to login
3. Enable policyholders to view their policy details
4. Calculate premiums based on term plans
5. Provide loan facilities for policyholders
6. Support online payment processing
7. Keep users informed about new strategies and subsidiary schemes

---

## 3. Scope of the Project

### In Scope:
- User registration and authentication
- Four types of insurance services:
  - Life Insurance
  - Medical Insurance
  - Motor Insurance
  - Home Insurance
- Policy information display
- Premium calculator based on term plans
- Loan facility for policyholders
- Online payment integration
- Admin panel for company officials

### Out of Scope:
- Mobile application
- Third-party insurance comparison
- Claim processing workflow
- Agent commission management

---

## 4. Target Users

1. **New Users:** Individuals seeking insurance information
2. **Registered Users:** People who want to apply for insurance
3. **Policyholders:** Existing customers with active policies
4. **Company Officials:** Insurance company staff managing the system
5. **Administrators:** System administrators

---

## 5. Expected Benefits

1. **For Customers:**
   - 24/7 access to policy information
   - Quick premium calculations
   - Easy online payments
   - Convenient loan applications

2. **For Insurance Company:**
   - Reduced manual paperwork
   - Faster policy processing
   - Better customer service
   - Centralized data management
   - Cost reduction in operations

---

## 6. Constraints

1. **Technical Constraints:**
   - Must work on Windows with IIS Server
   - Requires internet connectivity
   - Database size limitations

2. **Business Constraints:**
   - Must comply with insurance regulations
   - Data security and privacy requirements
   - Integration with existing payment gateways

3. **Time Constraints:**
   - Project completion within allocated timeframe
   - Regular status updates required

---

## 7. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:
- All four insurance types are implemented
- Users can register, login, and view policies
- Premium calculator works accurately
- Loan facility is functional
- Online payment is integrated
- Complete documentation is provided
- System is tested and bug-free
